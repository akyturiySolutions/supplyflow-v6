About the Book
A story two decades in the making

John Mwaura grew up shaped by the discipline of a father who trained as a teacher at Kilimambogo -- a place that produced educators who believed learning was the foundation of everything. That belief never left him.

Over more than twenty years working across the evolving landscape of ICT in Kenya, Mwaura built websites for schools and churches when the internet was still a novelty, taught primary school children how to use computers and write code when most adults did not yet own one, and helped local businesses embrace digital tools that would have seemed like science fiction a decade earlier.

"Technology is not something that happens to Africa. It is something Africa can lead."

Today he is a leading voice in AI literacy, climate-smart innovation, and digital empowerment for Kenyan families and institutions. This memoir traces that entire journey -- the failures, the discoveries, the classrooms, the code, and the conviction that a child of a teacher has a responsibility to keep teaching.

Part personal history, part field guide for the digital age, A Child of a Teacher speaks to entrepreneurs, educators, parents, and anyone who believes that where you start does not determine where you can go.

About the Author
John Mwaura

John Mwaura is a Kenyan technologist, community educator, and digital transformation advocate. For more than two decades he has worked across the evolving landscape of ICT -- from early web development for schools and churches to helping local businesses embrace modern digital tools.

Driven by the discipline passed down from his father, a teacher trained at Kilimambogo, Mwaura continues to champion technology as a tool for empowerment, resilience, and opportunity for Kenyan families and institutions.

Today he builds AI-powered commerce platforms for small businesses, promotes AI literacy, and advocates for climate-smart digital innovation across East Africa.
